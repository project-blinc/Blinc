# Changelog

All notable changes to `blinc_cn` will be documented in this file.

## [Unreleased]

### Fixed
- `cn::table` text is readable in dark mode: the table sets `color: var(--text-primary)`, which cells inherit, where cell text used to fall back to black. Header and caption text are muted by the `.cn-table-head` and `.cn-table-caption` rules instead of a colour picked in Rust, header and footer sit on `--surface-overlay` by rule, and body rows highlight with a wash of the text colour. `TableRow::selected` takes a bool, Signal or Computed and shows it as `data-state="selected"`; `.cn-table-row--selected` is gone.
- Turning a `cn::switch` off no longer snaps: the thumb keeps its place while the switch is held and eases back on release, and the track fades from its on colour to its off colour instead of jumping. Hovering an off track lightens it (a wash of the text colour over the idle fill), where it used to darken.
- The combobox's option rows clip to the popup, so a long list no longer paints below it or over the search field while scrolled.
- Clicking the trigger of an open `cn::dropdown_menu` or `cn::popover` closes it. The press counted as a click outside, which closed the menu, and the click that followed reopened it; the trigger is now inside the click-outside set.

### Added
- `cn::calendar`: a month of days to pick a date from, bound to a `Signal<Option<CalendarDay>>`. Month and year selects between previous and next buttons, the week's days, and six weeks in a grid; days of the months either side are muted, today is marked, the selected day filled. A turning month slides in from its side by the `cn-calendar-*` keyframes; dragging or a two-finger swipe across the days moves the month with it, turning one month when let go far enough or springing back (momentum after the lift moves nothing, the next swipe follows at once, and the pointer leaving the days cancels a swipe or drag), while vertical scrolling over it still moves the page. A month is a `.cn-calendar-page` holding its grid. After a click the arrows, Page Up/Down, Home/End and Enter/Space work the days. `.week_start(..)`, `.min_year(..)`, `.max_year(..)`, `.today(..)` and `.on_change(..)` configure it. `.events(..)` takes a list, Signal or Computed of `CalendarEvent`s (a day, a title, a `kind` and an optional icon) and marks their days in place, a dot or the icon per event, coloured by `.cn-calendar-event[data-kind]` rules. `CalendarDay` does the date arithmetic and reads today's local date; `events_on` lists a day's events. The look is the `.cn-calendar*` rules.
- `cn::select`'s list scrolls past 300px and opens with the chosen option in the middle. `.virtual_rows(row_height)` builds only the rows in view, for lists of hundreds.
- The shown value of `cn::select` and `cn::combobox` stays on one line, and their triggers pad only the sides; a label measured a fraction narrower than it drew used to wrap its last letter onto a second, clipped line.
- `cn::number_gauge` and `cn::number_range_gauge`: a number, or an interval, shown as a fill tinted from the primary with the formatted value centred on it and a six-dot grip at each edge. The value is a `Signal<f64>`, or one `Signal<RangeInclusive<f64>>` for the range gauge; bounds are `.range(a..=b)`; `.disabled(..)` takes a bool, Signal or Computed and follows it in place. A press seeks and a drag follows; the wheel steps; once pressed, the arrows, Page Up/Down and Home/End move it. Values snap to the step. The width holds the widest formatted value and does not change with the value. The range gauge moves the grip nearer the pointer, alternates between coincident grips, and never lets the ends cross. Colours, border, radius and cursor are `.cn-number-gauge` rules; `GaugeSize` sets the height. Both are in the prelude.
- `cn::dropdown_menu` is keyboard-driven: Down and Up move through the enabled rows (wrapping, skipping separators and disabled rows), Home and End jump to the first and last, Enter and Space activate the active row. The active row is the `.cn-dropdown-item--active` class and takes the hover fill. An open submenu receives the keys instead.
- The carets of `cn::select`, `cn::combobox`, the default `cn::dropdown_menu` trigger and `cn::accordion` turn half a turn when opened, eased by a `transition` on `.cn-chevron` keyed on the trigger's `data-state="open"`.
- `cn::number_input` steps with the wheel: one wheel line (40 units) is one step, wheel up increasing; trackpad deltas accumulate to a whole step; reversing direction or leaving the control clears the remainder; horizontal gestures and a disabled control are ignored.

### Changed
- **Breaking:** `cn::collapsible`, `cn::accordion`, `cn::tree_view` and `cn::sidebar` open and close by rule and ease by the theme's motion tokens. Their content and triggers carry `data-state="open"`/`"closed"` (the sidebar `"expanded"`/`"collapsed"`); `.cn-collapsible-content`, `.cn-accordion-content` and `.cn-tree-group` lay a closed section out at no height, and the size change and the sections below ease over `--duration-normal` on `--ease-out` instead of a spring. Chevrons turn by rule. `CollapsibleBuilder::{scale_anim, opacity_anim}`, `CollapsibleWithContent` and `AccordionBuilder::spring` are removed, `CollapsibleTrigger::new` takes the label and signal only, and `AccordionBuilder::item_with_state` takes a `Signal<bool>`. `collapsible_section` grows by height like the rest rather than scaling and fading, and its look is the `.cn-collapsible-section` and `.cn-collapsible-trigger` rules.
- **Breaking:** `cn::tree_view` is rebuilt. Each `.cn-tree-item` holds a `.cn-tree-row` (a `.cn-tree-chevron` or `.cn-tree-chevron-space`, an optional `.cn-tree-icon`, a `.cn-tree-label`) and, with children, a `.cn-tree-group` indented beside a `.cn-tree-guide` line. Rows carry `data-selected`, `data-diff` (`added`, `removed`, `modified`) and `data-focus-visible`; the colours, hover, selected fill, diff colours and focus ring are those rules, and nothing is coloured in Rust. Guides show by default (`.guides(false)` hides them, `.cn-tree[data-guides]` draws them); `.indent(px)` sets a group's inset, 14px by default. Once the tree is clicked, Up/Down walk the shown rows, Right opens a row or enters it, Left closes it or goes to its parent, Home/End jump, and Enter/Space select. `.cn-tree-node`, `.cn-tree-node--selected` and `.cn-tree-children` are gone.
- **Breaking:** `cn::sidebar` collapses by a CSS `width` transition. The rail's width is `var(--cn-sidebar-width, 200px)`, and `var(--cn-sidebar-collapsed-width, 41px)` under `[data-state="collapsed"]`; the content beside it, in a `.cn-sidebar-inset` within a `.cn-sidebar-layout`, is laid out again each frame and moves with it. Labels (`.cn-sidebar-label`) fade and group titles (`.cn-sidebar-group-label`) fold away by rule, and the active item carries `data-active` (its `.cn-sidebar-icon` turns primary). The layout animations on the rail, its items and the content are gone, as are `SidebarBuilder::{expanded_width, collapsed_width}`, which had no effect; set widths with rules. `.cn-sidebar-item--active` is gone.
- `cn::pie_chart()`: slices by their share of the total, clockwise from the top, sweeping in and easing to new values; `.donut(hole)` with `.total(caption)` writes the total in the hole. Hovering pulls a slice out with a tooltip of its value and share. Slices are `.slice(label, value)` or `.data(..)` of `PieSlice`, coloured from the palette like series, and set apart by `--cn-chart-surface`; the parts are the chart rules plus `.cn-chart-pie-total` and `.cn-chart-pie-caption`.
- Charts draw their bars, dots, lines, grid, cursor and hover band as SDF primitives (rects, circles and capsules along the flattened curve), so their edges are smooth on any target; only area fills are tessellated paths.
- **Breaking:** `cn::bar_chart()` moves onto the shared plot: series side by side in each category, piled with `.stacked()`, or along the width with `.horizontal()`, rounded at the end away from the axis, with a band behind the category under the pointer and the same tooltip, legend, easing and colours as the line and area charts. It takes `.series(..)`, `.data(..)` and `.labels(..)` like them; `BarChart`, `BarChartBuilder`, `.data(&[(&str, f64)])`, `.color(..)`, `.gap(..)`, `.no_labels()` and `.build()` are gone.
- **Breaking:** `cn::line_chart()` is rebuilt on a canvas and is interactive, and `cn::area_chart()` is new. Moving the pointer across a chart draws a cursor at the category under it, enlarges its dots and shows a tooltip of each series' value there, on the side with room. Series are `.series(name, values)`, or `.data(..)` with a list, Signal or Computed of `ChartSeries`, which the chart follows by easing from the old values to the new; `.labels(..)` likewise. Smooth (the default), linear and stepped curves, stacking, dots, a dashed grid, value-axis ticks at round numbers, category labels that thin out when they would crowd, and a legend for more than one series. Colours are `--chart-1` to `--chart-5` and `--cn-chart-{grid,cursor,surface}`, and the parts are `.cn-chart*` rules. `LineChart`, `LineChartBuilder`, `DataPoint`, `DataSeries`, `ChartGrid`, `.with_dots()`, `.stroke_width(..)` and `.build()` are gone. The other chart kinds are unchanged for now.
- **Breaking:** `CN_STYLES` sets the document's text colour, `:root { color: var(--text-primary) }`. Text and icons that set no colour and sit in nothing that does take it, where they were black.
- **Breaking:** `cn::icon` without a colour takes the text colour around it, as `currentColor` does, instead of always `--text-primary`; `.color(..)` and `.color_value(..)` still set one.
- **Breaking:** toasts and avatars take their colours from rules. A toast carries `data-variant` (`default`, `success`, `warning`, `destructive`), and success, warning and destructive toasts colour their border and `.cn-toast-icon` by it; its parts are `.cn-toast-title`, `.cn-toast-description` and `.cn-toast-close`, and it looks as before. `.cn-toast--{info,success,warning,error}` are gone. An avatar's initials are coloured by `.cn-avatar` (`--cn-avatar-fg`), its placeholder is `.cn-avatar-placeholder`, its status dot `.cn-avatar-status[data-status]`, and a group's ring and "+N" chip are `.cn-avatar-group` rules; `.fallback_bg(..)`, `.fallback_color(..)` and `.ring(..)` still override them.
- Card titles and descriptions, keyboard keys, separators, field messages and resizable handles take their colours from rules. The new parts are `.cn-card-title`, `.cn-card-description`, `.cn-field-error`, `.cn-field-description` and `.cn-resizable-line`; a separator carries `data-orientation`, and a handle being dragged `data-state="dragging"` (its line also turns `--primary` on hover). The key's border, radius and shadow moved to `.cn-kbd`. `cn::input` leaves its text, placeholder, caret and selection colours to the field's theme defaults unless set.
- **Breaking:** breadcrumbs, pagination and tab triggers take their text colours and weights from rules; nothing is coloured in Rust. A breadcrumb link carries `data-link` (and turns `--text-primary` on hover, where it turned `--primary`), the last step `aria-current="page"` with weight 500, and the separators are `.cn-breadcrumb-separator`; a step without a click handler before the last is no longer drawn as the current page. A pagination button for the current page carries `aria-current="page"` and an arrow with nowhere to go `disabled`; the gaps are `.cn-pagination-ellipsis`. A tab trigger's text and icon follow `.cn-tabs-trigger` (`--text-secondary`, `--text-primary` on hover) and `.cn-tabs-trigger--active` (`--text-primary`, weight 500). `.cn-breadcrumb-item--active`, `.cn-pagination-btn--active` and `.cn-pagination-btn--disabled` are gone.
- **Breaking:** `cn::progress` takes its colours and motion from rules. The bar's `flex-grow` is bound to the value and eases to each new one by the `transition` on `.cn-progress-bar` (`--duration-slow`, `--ease-nav`); the track carries `data-size`. `cn::progress_indeterminate()` is new: part of the track pulses by the `cn-progress-pulse` keyframes, keyed on `data-indeterminate`. `progress_animated` and `AnimatedProgress` are removed, since a bound value eases by rule; `.indicator_color(..)`, `.track_color(..)` and `.rounded(..)` still override the rules for one bar. `.cn-progress--{sm,md,lg}` are gone.
- **Breaking:** `cn::slider` is rebuilt. It is a `.cn-slider` row of `.cn-slider-fill`, `.cn-slider-thumb` and `.cn-slider-rest`, the fill and rest bound to the value so the thumb follows it in place, with a `.cn-slider-field` and `.cn-slider-header` (`.cn-slider-label`, `.cn-slider-value`) around it when labelled. Every colour, the thumb's ring and shadow, and its growth on hover and while held are rules; the disabled look keys on the `disabled` attribute. `.disabled(..)` takes a bool, Signal or Computed and is followed in place. A press on the track seeks and one on the thumb holds it where it was grabbed; the wheel steps it, and once pressed the arrows, Page Up/Down and Home/End move it. Sizes follow the other range controls (thumb 12, 16 or 22px). `track_color`, `fill_color`, `thumb_color` and `Slider::id` are removed (use `--cn-slider-track-bg`, `--cn-slider-fill-bg`, `--cn-slider-thumb-bg`), as are the hover halo, the thumb spring, `.cn-slider-track`, `.cn-slider-thumb--disabled`, and `bind_number_target`, `num_binding` and `NumberValue::bind_target`, which only the slider used.
- **Breaking:** `cn::tabs` marks the active trigger with a pill, `.cn-tabs-indicator`, that slides to the next trigger and takes its width on a switch; the fill and shadow moved there from `.cn-tabs-trigger--active`. The default `TabsTransition` is the new `Slide`: panels slide the way the pill moved, by `data-direction="next"` or `"prev"` on each panel.
- **Breaking:** dialogs, sheets, drawers, toasts and tab panels enter and leave by CSS `@keyframes` rules instead of Rust animations. Each one's wrapper carries `data-motion="enter"`, then `"exit"` while it leaves, and stays until its exit has played; the rules are `.cn-dialog-motion`, `.cn-sheet-motion-{side}`, `.cn-drawer-motion-{side}`, `.cn-toast-motion-{side}` and `.cn-tabs-panel[data-transition]`, with the backdrop's fade on `.overlay-backdrop`, all timed by the theme's motion tokens. A sheet or drawer slides by its own size, a dialog rises into place, a toast slides from its side. `DialogBuilder::{enter_animation, exit_animation}`, `SheetBuilder::{enter_animation, exit_animation, animation_duration}` and `DrawerBuilder::animation_duration` are removed: a different motion is a rule of your own.
- **Breaking:** `cn::spinner` turns and `cn::skeleton` pulses by CSS `@keyframes` (`cn-spinner-turn` on `.cn-spinner__arc`, `cn-skeleton-pulse` on `.cn-skeleton`), timed and coloured by rules; they no longer run a Rust timeline. `SpinnerBuilder::duration_ms`, `Skeleton::shimmer` and `AnimatedSkeleton` are removed: a different speed is `animation-duration` in a rule of your own. Every skeleton pulses now. The spinner's `.color(..)` and `.track_color(..)` still override the rules for one spinner.
- Menus share one set of parts and rules: the dropdown, context and menubar menus build their panel, rows, shortcuts, submenu chevrons and separators from the same pieces, classed `.cn-menu`, `.cn-menu-item` (a `disabled` attribute when disabled), `.cn-menu-item-label`, `.cn-menu-shortcut`, `.cn-menu-chevron` and `.cn-menu-separator`, beside their own `.cn-dropdown-*`, `.cn-context-menu-*` and `.cn-menubar-*` classes. Their colours, the disabled look and the hover highlight are those rules; nothing is coloured in Rust. `.cn-dropdown-item--disabled` is gone (use `.cn-menu-item:disabled`). The navigation menu's links and panel, the menubar's bar and the default dropdown trigger take their look from rules as well.
- Overlay panels (tooltip, popover, hover card, select and combobox lists, menus) follow the theme's corner shape tokens like every other rounded element; they were held to circular corners.
- Overlay panels take their whole look from `CN_STYLES`: the tooltip, popover, hover card, dialog, sheet and drawer panels, and the select and combobox lists, no longer set fill, border, radius, shadow or text colour in Rust, so a rule of your own reaches them. Panels sit on `--surface-elevated` with token shadows (`--shadow-sm` tooltip, `--shadow-md` popover, hover card and lists, `--shadow-xl` dialog, sheet and drawer). Dialog, sheet and drawer titles and descriptions are `.cn-{dialog,sheet,drawer}-title` / `-description`, their close buttons `.cn-sheet-close` / `.cn-drawer-close` (tinting on hover), and their separators `-separator`. A select or combobox option is `disabled` as an attribute (`.cn-select-item:disabled`), and the selected option's accent colour now shows; the combobox trigger carries `data-placeholder` while nothing is chosen. The chevrons' colour is a rule.
- `cn::select`'s shown text takes its colour from the trigger: `.cn-select-trigger` sets it, and `[data-placeholder]` (nothing chosen) and `:disabled` dim it. The text is no longer coloured in Rust, so a rule of your own on the trigger reaches it.
- **Breaking:** `cn::checkbox`, `cn::switch` and `cn::toggle` take a `Signal<bool>` instead of a `&State<bool>`, and `cn::radio_group`, `cn::toggle_group`, `cn::select`, `cn::combobox` and `cn::input_otp` a `Signal<String>` (the OTP input's `.error(..)` binds too), and `cn::number_input` a `Signal<f64>`. `cn::slider` takes a `Signal<f32>` or `Signal<f64>` (`NumberValue` now holds signals), and `cn::pagination` any `impl Into<PageValue>`: a `Signal<usize>`, `Signal<i32>` or float signal. `cn::input` and `cn::textarea` take a bound `.disabled(..)` and `.label(..)` as well. `cn::label`'s `.disabled(..)` binds the same way. `.disabled(..)` and `.label(..)` take a value, Signal or Computed and follow it in place. The label's colour is a rule (`.cn-switch-label`, dimmed under `.cn-switch-row:disabled`); the unused `cn-checkbox--disabled` and `cn-switch--disabled` classes are gone, and `.cn-switch:disabled` carries the dimming.
- `cn::input`, `cn::textarea`, the OTP slots and `cn::number_input` carry `box-shadow: var(--shadow-sm)`, as the select, combobox and outline button already did, and none while disabled; a disabled number input also takes the disabled fill.
- `cn::number_input`'s wheel stepping lives in a module shared with the gauges; its behaviour is unchanged.
- `cn::number_input` is one bordered group (`.cn-number-input-group`) with a borderless field and two steps whose fill, divider and icon colour are `CN_STYLES` rules. A step tints on hover and press, and is `disabled` (dimmed, inert) where a step would change nothing. The pointer-signal colour code is gone.
- Shadows, motion and shape come from theme tokens in the rules: the select, combobox and dropdown triggers take `box-shadow: var(--shadow-sm)` (and `none` while disabled) from `CN_STYLES` instead of `.shadow_sm()` in Rust; the input, textarea and OTP focus-ring transitions use `--duration-fast` and `--ease-state` instead of literal times. `.cn-slider-thumb` now has `var(--shadow-sm)` (none while disabled); it was flat.
- `cn::combobox`: the trigger's border on hover, while open and while disabled is `.cn-combobox-trigger` rules (`data-state="open"`, `disabled`) with a `transition`, instead of a colour computed from pointer signals in Rust; a disabled combobox now shows the disabled palette. The search field takes its fill and border from `.cn-combobox-search` rules; the `.hover_*` and `.focused_*` colour setters it called are gone.
- `cn::select` and the default `cn::dropdown_menu` trigger: fill and border on hover, press, open and disabled are `CN_STYLES` rules (`.cn-select-trigger` with `data-state="open"` and `disabled` attributes, `.cn-dropdown-trigger`) with a `transition`, instead of colours computed from pointer signals in Rust.
- `cn::textarea`: border and fill per state are `CN_STYLES` rules (`.cn-textarea`, `.cn-textarea--error`) on the layout rules, eased by a `transition`; `.error(..)` now adds `cn-textarea--error`, so the border is red before anything is typed.
- `cn::input`: border and fill while hovered, focused, in error and disabled are `CN_STYLES` rules (`.cn-input`, `.cn-input--error`) on top of the layout input's `.blinc-input` rules, eased by a `transition`; the Rust colour code is gone. `hover_border_color`, `focused_border_color`, `error_border_color`, `border_colors`, `hover_bg_color`, `focused_bg_color`, `bg_colors`, `InputBorderColors` and `InputBgColors` are removed; use rules. `idle_border_color` and `idle_bg_color` set inline style. `.error(..)` adds `cn-input--error`, so the border is red before anything is typed. `cn::input_otp` slots and the combobox search field take their look from rules the same way.
- `cn::badge`: a bound label inherits its `color` from the `.cn-badge--{style}-{variant}` rule, like a static one, instead of setting it in Rust from a table that mirrored the stylesheet. `cn::radio_group` takes its look from the layout radio's `.blinc-radio` rules; `CN_STYLES` keeps only its custom-property hooks.
- `cn::toggle` and `cn::toggle_group` items are one element each with `data-state`, `data-variant`, `data-size` and `disabled` attributes; their look, the variant and size ladder, and hover, press and on states are the `.blinc-toggle` rules of the layout stylesheet, with a `transition`. The Rust colour code, size tables passed per item and the copied `mix` helper are gone. A group sets `data-variant` and `data-size` for its items, and its default gap is 4px, not 16px.
- `cn::radio_group`: each ring is an element with `checked` and `disabled` attributes; border colour, the dot's fade-in, the disabled fill and hover are `CN_STYLES` rules, and the stylesheet-by-id override path is gone. `selected_color`, `border_color` and `hover_border_color` are removed; restyle with rules such as `.my-radio:checked { border-color: #16a34a }`. An unselected option now highlights on hover.
- `cn::checkbox` is an element with `checked` and `disabled` attributes: fill, border colour, hover, the disabled palette and the mark's fade-in are `CN_STYLES` rules with a `transition`. `checked_color`, `unchecked_bg`, `border_color`, `hover_border_color` and `check_color` are removed; restyle with rules such as `.my-checkbox:checked { background: #16a34a }`. A disabled checked box now shows its mark. It builds on `blinc_layout::widgets::checkbox`, so `.class(..)` and `.id(..)` apply to the box. The check mark is bolder and larger (11, 14 and 18px for the three sizes).
- `cn::switch` is an element with a `checked` attribute: its track fill (off, on, hover, disabled), the thumb's slide and its press shrink are `CN_STYLES` rules with a `transition`, per size (`.cn-switch--sm|md|lg`). The spring and the Rust colour setters (`on_color`, `off_color`, `thumb_color`, `spring`) are removed; restyle with rules, such as `.my-switch:checked { background: #16a34a }`. The thumb stays inside the track while it slides.
- `cn::button`: each variant's fill, text colour, border and shadow, and how they change on hover, press and while disabled, are `CN_STYLES` rules with a CSS `transition`, instead of colours computed in Rust. Rules for a hovered or pressed button leave a disabled one alone (`:not(:disabled)`). A pressed button that is still hovered keeps its hover colour, as on the web.

### Added
- **`cn::input_otp`** — themed segmented one-time-code input backed by linked single-character `text_input` slots. Supports auto-advance, empty-slot Backspace rewind, paste distribution across remaining slots, optional numeric-only filtering, masking, disabled state, error styling, and grouped separators. The shared slot behavior lives in `blinc_layout::widgets::input_otp`; cn contributes the themed border/background/focus surface.
- **`cn::table`** — themed wrapper around `blinc_layout::widgets::table` exposing the shadcn surface: `cn::table()` / `cn::table_header()` / `cn::table_body()` / `cn::table_footer()` / `cn::table_row()` / `cn::table_head(label)` / `cn::table_cell()` / `cn::table_caption(label)`. The layout widget already paints section backgrounds + cell padding + flex-1 cell distribution from theme tokens; cn only adds the surface CSS (1 px outer border at `--border`, `--radius-md`, `:hover` row tint at `--surface-hover`, `:last-child` border-strip so the bottom row sits flush with the rounded edge), the medium-weight muted-foreground header text, and the footer's top-border + medium font-weight. `cn::table_row().selected(true)` toggles the `.cn-table-row--selected` class which paints `--selection`. No sort / filter — that's the eventual `cn::data_grid`.
- **`cn::number_input`** — themed wrapper around the underlying `text_input` with a `+` / `−` SVG-icon stepper pair flanking the field. Reuses `InputSize` from `cn::input` for height / font alignment so number inputs sit flush with other form inputs of the matching size. Behaviour:
  - **Bound to a `State<f64>`** — stepper buttons clamp against `min` / `max` and call `state.set`. An outer `Stateful` watches `state.signal_id()` and re-renders the field on every value change so steppers / external code / keyboard stepping all push their updates through the visible field automatically (no `refresh_text_input` boilerplate at the caller).
  - **Centred value + tight cell** — `text_input::text_align(Center)` + `padding_x(4)` for a fixed-width cell that hugs the centred digit count. Default cell is 64 px; callers can override via `.w(px)` for a roomy six-digit field or a single-digit one.
  - **Keyboard stepping** — ↑ / ↓ / `+` / `−` step the value (via `text_input::on_step`).
  - **Click-to-select-all** — single-click on the centred field selects the whole value so the next keystroke replaces it (canonical `<input type=number>` UX).
  - **No mid-edit reformat** — while the field is focused, the outer Stateful skips the "format state into visible text" sync, so typing `30.0` doesn't get auto-canonicalised to `3.00.0` mid-stroke. Reformatting happens on blur or via stepper.
- **`cn::toggle_group`** — single-select toggle bar (shadcn's `<ToggleGroup type="single">`). Takes a `State<String>` for the active value; items are `cn::toggle_item(value).label(…).icon(…).disabled(…)`. Each item is a `Stateful<ButtonState>` watching the group's signal id, so clicks anywhere in the row re-render all items in lockstep. Inherits the same theme-token defaults + variant / size ladder as `cn::toggle` (the visual rules are re-resolved per item inside the callback). Multi-select (`type="multiple"`) is a follow-up that wants its own `State<Vec<String>>` overload.
- **`cn::toggle`** — shadcn-style binary toggle button. Themed wrapper around `blinc_layout::widgets::toggle`, contributing `.cn-toggle` + `.cn-toggle--default` / `.cn-toggle--outline` variant classes and `.cn-toggle--sm` / `--md` / `--lg` size classes. All parse / state / token-default work lives in the layout widget — cn only supplies the surface CSS and the variant / size selection. `ToggleVariant::Default` (no border off) is the toolbar-friendly default; `ToggleVariant::Outline` borders the off state (pairs with future `cn::toggle_group`). `ToggleSize::{Small, Medium, Large}` heights line up with `cn::button` and `cn::input` so toggle rows sit flush.
- **HID focus ring on `.cn-input` / `.cn-textarea`**. On focus the border brightens to `--border-focus` and a 2 px outer ring at 2 px offset draws around the input edge using a 35 %-alpha tint (`--focus-ring`) so the ring reads as a soft halo distinct from the crisp border. Error and success variants get their own `--focus-ring-error` / `--focus-ring-success` colours. Outline scales from a transparent 1 px-offset baseline to the focus 2 px offset with a 160 ms ease for the first focus interaction (see Known Issues).
- **Semantic easings wired through dialog / sheet / drawer / toast and the CSS surface**. Enter / exit animations now use `--ease-default` (or the variant-specific role) instead of the previous fixed `EaseInOut`. The cn stylesheet picks up the same vars so user-written CSS transitions match the framework's built-in motion.
- **`ButtonSize::Custom(width, height)`**. Escape hatch for cases where the Small / Medium / Large / Icon ladder doesn't fit — wide auth-flow CTAs, tight inline actions, settings rows aligned to a specific column.

### Changed
- `cn::number_input` now derives its field width from configured bounds and precision rather than the current value, so typing more digits does not resize the control. Keyboard step changes force one focused-field sync so the visible value tracks ↑ / ↓ / `+` / `−` immediately without reformatting ordinary typed input mid-edit.
- Dropdown menu / context menu / select / combobox / menubar / nav-menu hover highlights now clip to the panel's outer radius, so the highlight follows the panel's rounded edge instead of leaving a visible strip of the panel's surface bg at the corners.
- Combobox search input bumps `radius_sm` → `radius_md` to match the dropdown's corner reach, plus added horizontal padding so the input doesn't butt against the panel edge.
- Breadcrumb item labels + separators (slash / text) now `.no_wrap()` so long path segments don't fold across two lines mid-trail.
- `cn::label` collapses to `w_fit()` and the inner text is `.no_wrap()` so a long label doesn't take the full row width.
- `.cn-input` / `.cn-textarea` no longer redeclare `border:` at the base level — the layout `TextInput` setters supply idle / hover / focused border colours; the redundant base rule was being rewritten by `apply_complex_selector_styles` every frame and clobbering the setter-chosen focused colour.
- `apply_css_overrides` on text input / text area now applies `:focus` AFTER `:hover` for the `FocusedHovered` state so focus colour wins while the user is typing in a hovered input. Same ordering applied to the outline-extraction path.
- All component `.class()` builders take `impl AsRef<str>` (was `impl Into<String>`), and per-component `classes` / `css_classes` storage is now `Vec<Arc<str>>` interned through `blinc_core::intern`. A class repeated across hundreds of nodes now allocates exactly once.
- `element_classes()` overrides return `&[Arc<str>]` to match the trait change in `blinc_layout`.

### Fixed
- Popover / context-menu / dropdown / select / combobox / menubar / dialog / sheet / drawer / toast animations now play on the first interaction (class-only `@keyframes` rules were previously skipped on the very first build).
- Dropdown menu's top/bottom Rust-side `.py(1.0)` padding removed (was double-padding with the CSS `padding:` declaration).

### Known Issues
- `.cn-input` / `.cn-textarea` focus ring transition plays smoothly on the first focus but snaps in on every subsequent focus. Border-color transition is unaffected; only the outline transition is affected. See `gotcha_focus_ring_transition_no_replay.md` in dev memory. Deferred.
